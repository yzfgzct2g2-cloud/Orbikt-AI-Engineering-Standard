## Versioning Rules for v2

- The v2 specification documents carry their own semantic versions in their headers
  (VER-01…VER-05 semantics of the v1 lineage are retained as practice).
- Whole-standard releases are recorded in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) and tagged in
  git as `v<MAJOR>.<MINOR>.<PATCH>` from v2.0.0 onward. Released tags are never
  moved.
